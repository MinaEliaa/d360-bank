Questions, answered.

What is D360 Bank?

D360 Bank is a Saudi digital bank headquartered in Riyadh and licensed by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA). Accounts, cards, transfers and savings all run from the D360 app, and every product is Shariah-compliant.

How long does it take to open a D360 account?

About 2 minutes. You open your account entirely in the D360 app, with no branch visit.

Is D360 Shariah-compliant?

Yes. 100% of D360's products and services are Shariah-compliant and are overseen by the bank's Shariah committee.

Which cards does D360 offer?

The Debit Card for paying in store, online and abroad with no fees in any currency; the Masareef prepaid card for your family, with spending limits you set in the app; the Ehsan Card, where every payment supports a good cause; and the Pink Card.

Does D360 charge hidden fees?

No. D360 has 0 SAR hidden fees, international transfers carry no hidden fee, and salaries sent through Musaned cost 0 SAR.

What savings and financing rates does D360 offer?

Sanabil savings earn 3% annual profit, and personal financing is offered at a 4.49% annual percentage rate (APR).
